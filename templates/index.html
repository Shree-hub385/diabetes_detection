What this project does

Implements the Section 4 plan of the capstone: adopt & modify the base paper's approach.

Pipeline

  • Data: Pima Indians Diabetes (768 patients, 8 predictors). Impossible zeros (glucose, BP, skin-fold, insulin, BMI) are treated as missing and median-imputed inside the pipeline (no leakage).
  • Benchmark: the base paper's 5 classifiers re-run on an identical stratified 80:20 split.
  • Imbalance: class weighting and SMOTE (applied to training folds only) — evaluated as an ablation and as part of the tuning search.
  • Tuning: randomized search (5-fold CV, F1) for Random Forest and XGBoost.
  • Stacking: RF + XGBoost with a Logistic-Regression meta-learner.
  • Final model is chosen by cross-validated ROC-AUC on the training set only — never on the test set.

Explainability

  • Each score is accompanied by SHAP values: red bars push the predicted risk up, blue bars push it down, relative to the average training patient.
  • The risk bands (low < 35 %, moderate 35–65 %, high ≥ 65 %) are for display; use the threshold slider to explore the sensitivity / specificity trade-off.

Limitations (inherited from the base paper)

  • 768 records from one population subgroup; may not generalise.
  • No independent external test set.
  • Associations, not causation. Not a medical device.
